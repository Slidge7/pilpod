## 1. Executive Summary & Core Positioning

### What is PilPod?
**PilPod** is the ultimate desktop media command center and multi-browser controller for Windows. It pulls every playing video, music stream, audio session, and open browser tab across all your browsers and desktop apps into one unified, ultra-responsive control panel.

### The Two Components
1. **PilPod Desktop App (The Mothership):**
   A high-performance, native Windows application with a stunning frosted-glass aesthetic. It controls system-wide audio, manages media playlists, hosts a dedicated distraction-free video player, organizes bookmarks in an offline vault, and features an always-available corner widget.
2. **PilPod Companion (The Browser Extension):**
   A featherweight browser extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Opera GX, Vivaldi, and other Chromium browsers. It acts as the "eyes and ears" inside your web browser, instantly transmitting media states, titles, artworks, and playback controls to the PilPod desktop app via secure local communication.

### Core Brand Philosophy
* **Control Without Context Switching:** You never have to leave your current game, IDE, design canvas, or document to pause a video, skip a song, boost quiet speech, or mute a loud tab.
* **Tame the Multi-Tab Chaos:** Built specifically for people who keep dozens (or hundreds) of tabs open across multiple browsers and profiles.
* **Total Privacy by Design:** 100% offline, zero cloud servers, zero analytics, zero data harvesting. What you watch and listen to stays exclusively on your PC.

## 2. Target Audience & Use Cases

* **Power Users & Tab Hoarders:** Users who routinely run 30+ tabs across Chrome, Edge, and Brave, and frequently lose track of which tab is playing audio or where that background video is running.
* **Software Developers & Designers:** Professionals working across full-screen code editors or design tools who want instant media controls, volume boosting, and playlist management without alt-tabbing away from their workflow.
* **Gamers & Streamers:** Players in full-screen games who need a discreet floating corner widget or global hotkeys to adjust background YouTube/Spotify volume, mute audio during voice chat, or skip songs without minimizing games.
* **Students & Researchers:** People studying from multiple video lectures, webinars, and audio sources who need volume boosting on quiet recordings, seamless bookmarking, and custom cross-platform playlists.
* **Music & Podcast Enthusiasts:** Listeners who want to build unified playlists containing tracks from YouTube, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, and web radios without being tied to a single platform.

## 5. Key Feature Showcases (Detailed User Benefits)

### 5.1 The Universal Media Command Center
* **What it does:** Automatically detects and displays every active audio or video stream running in any browser or desktop player.
* **Supported Platforms:** Works natively on YouTube, YouTube Music, Spotify Web, Netflix, Twitch, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Vimeo, Prime Video, Disney+, Apple Music, Kick, and any HTML5 audio/video stream on the internet.
* **Full Transport Controls:**
  * Play / Pause toggle with immediate response.
  * **Precision Timeline Scrubbing:** Drag the seek bar to jump through long videos or podcasts without switching to the browser tab.
  * **Picture-in-Picture (PiP) in 1 Click:** Pop out any playing video into a floating always-on-top window.
  * **Track Navigation:** Skip forward and backward on supported media services.
  * **Tab Lifecycle Actions:** Reload sluggish tabs, close finished tabs, or click "Open in Browser" to bring the exact browser window to the front.
* **The Active Media Strip:** An animated, smart-sorting strip at the top of the app that keeps currently playing media pinned and organized, preventing erratic jumping while you interact.

### 5.2 Volume Boost & Global Emergency Audio Controls
* **Individual Tab Volume Sliders:** Adjust the volume of each tab independently from 0% to 150%.
* **Volume Boost (100% – 150%):** Hear quiet lecturers, muffled audiobooks, or low-gain videos loud and clear without having to turn up your master speakers.
* **Visual Sound Level Feedback:** Sliders glow distinctly across muted, standard, boosted, and high ranges.
* **Emergency Quick Actions:**
  * **Pause All:** Immediately pauses every playing media stream across all connected browsers.
  * **Mute All:** Silences all tab audio instantly without stopping playback.
  * **Reset All Volumes:** Returns all custom volume boosts back to default 100% with a single click.

### 5.3 Multi-Browser & Multi-Profile Tab Hub
* **Unified Browser Cockpit:** Run Google Chrome for personal use, Microsoft Edge for enterprise, and Brave or Opera GX for gaming — PilPod communicates with all of them concurrently.
* **Profile-Aware Organization:** Distinguishes between different browser profiles (e.g., Personal vs. Work) with original profile icons and labels.
* **Window-Grouped Tabs:** View your open tabs neatly grouped by OS window so you can easily understand your workspace layout.
* **Sleep & Crash Awareness:** Displays clear status badges for sleeping (frozen/discarded), loading, crashed, or active tabs.
* **1-Click Tab Reactivation:** Instantly wake up a suspended or sleeping tab without hunting for it in your browser.
* **Direct Window Focus:** Clicking any tab in PilPod immediately brings that specific browser window to the foreground.

### 5.4 The Floating Screen Widget ("The Chip")
* **Subtle, Always-Available Presence:** When you minimize PilPod, you can choose to tuck it into a discreet, minimalist corner widget known as **"The Chip"**.
* **Flexible Placement:**
  * **Corner Triangle:** Hugs any of your 4 screen corners (Top-Left, Top-Right, Bottom-Left, Bottom-Right) seamlessly.
  * **Floating Bubble:** Floats anywhere on your desktop along your screen edges.
* **Zero Disruption Workflow:**
  * Hover or glance to check if media is playing.
  * Click to instantly reveal the full PilPod command center.
  * Drag to reposition easily.
* **Ultra-Lightweight Performance:** Built with native, lightweight technology designed to sit on screen for days without using CPU or memory.

### 5.5 Universal Playlist Player & Dedicated In-App Video Stage
* **Cross-Platform Web Playlists:** Create and organize custom media playlists combining videos and tracks from YouTube, SoundCloud, web streams, and articles.
* **Two Playback Modes:**
  1. **Background Browser Playback:** PilPod drives playback through a single dedicated browser tab. It loads next tracks, handles repeats, and shuffles without ever stealing your screen focus.
  2. **Dedicated In-App Player Window:** A clean, distraction-free desktop window featuring an embedded video stage and an interactive playlist track list.
* **Distraction-Free Video Stage:** Watch YouTube videos without algorithmic recommendations, comments, ads clutter, or sidebar distractions.
* **Full Playlist Transport:** Drag-and-drop track reordering, repeat (off / repeat one / repeat all), shuffle mode, and automated track transition.

### 5.6 The Vault: Smart Bookmarks & Cross-Browser Collections
* **Your Personal Offline Media Vault:** Save any tab, video, article, or audio track into a persistent, searchable desktop vault.
* **Smart Tab Capture:**
  * **Save Current Tab:** Quick-add with one click.
  * **Save All Open Tabs:** Instantly archive an entire research session across 20 tabs into a named collection before closing your browser.
  * **Save All Playing Media:** Save your currently playing tracks into a new playlist in seconds.
* **Collections & Custom Tagging:** Group bookmarks with custom titles, emojis, tags, and personal markdown notes.
* **URL Normalization & Deduplication:** Prevents duplicate bookmarks even if URLs contain tracking query parameters from different browsers.
* **Backup & Restore:** Full JSON export and import so your bookmarks and playlists are always safely backed up on your local drive.

### 5.7 Instant Tab & Audio Search Hub
* **Find Any Tab in Milliseconds:** Type in the search box to search instantly across all open tabs, page titles, URLs, artists, albums, and track names.
* **Smart Filter Chips:** Filter your tabs by site (e.g., `youtube.com`, `github.com`, `reddit.com`) or by browser profile (e.g., `Work Chrome`, `Personal Edge`) with live count badges.
* **Instant Action from Search:** Play/pause, adjust volume, focus the window, or close tabs right from the search results.

### 5.8 Modern Liquid Glass Design, Custom Wallpapers & Idle Spotlight
* **Modern Windows Aesthetics:** Designed to blend naturally with Windows 11 Fluent and Acrylic design languages.
* **Liquid Frosted Glass:** Translucent, high-performance blurred glass panels that elevate your desktop workspace.
* **Adjustable Glass Strength:** Fine-tune the transparency and blur intensity with a dedicated slider.
* **Dynamic Wallpapers & Slideshows:**
  * Select from built-in atmospheric gradients and backgrounds.
  * Or point PilPod to your personal wallpaper folder on your computer.
  * Configurable auto-rotation intervals (5m, 30m, 1h, 4h, 12h, 24h) with smooth crossfade transitions.
* **Smart Idle Mode & Idle Spotlight:**
  * When you are inactive, PilPod gently dims into a quiet, elegant background state to save visual distraction.
  * Moving your mouse activates the **Idle Spotlight**, which softly illuminates your active media cards first so you can immediately see what’s playing.
* **Dark & Light Mode:** Seamless switching between deep obsidian dark mode and crisp modern light mode.

### 5.9 The PilPod Companion: Seamless Browser Bridge
* **How It Works:** A secure, zero-config extension that connects to the PilPod desktop app over local computer memory (`127.0.0.1`).
* **Effortless Onboarding:** PilPod Desktop automatically scans your PC, detects your installed browsers, and provides a 1-click guide to install the companion from the Chrome Web Store.
* **Intelligent Media Detection:** Captures title, artist, album art, duration, playback state, and volume without slowing down web page rendering.
* **Universal Compatibility:** Optimized for Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Opera, Opera GX, Brave, Vivaldi, and all Chromium-based browsers.

### 5.10 100% Local-First & Zero Telemetry Guarantee
* **No Cloud Accounts Required:** No signup, no login, no passwords, no email verification.
* **Zero Telemetry & Zero Tracking:** No analytics scripts, no tracking cookies, no advertising beacons.
* **Pure Localhost Communication:** All communication between the companion extension and desktop app happens strictly on your local machine. Nothing ever leaves your device.
* **Your Data Stays Yours:** Playlists, bookmarks, and preferences are stored in local files under your personal Windows user profile.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the difference between PilPod Desktop and PilPod Companion?
**PilPod Desktop** is the main standalone Windows application where you manage your media, volume, playlists, bookmarks, and floating widget. **PilPod Companion** is a lightweight browser extension that lets the desktop app detect and control media playing in your web tabs.

### Do I need both the Desktop App and the Browser Extension?
To get the full multi-browser control experience, **yes**. The Desktop App acts as your command cockpit, and the Companion Extension connects your browsers to that cockpit. Installing the companion takes just one click from the Chrome Web Store.

### Which browsers are supported?
PilPod Companion supports all major Chromium-based web browsers, including:
* Google Chrome
* Microsoft Edge
* Brave Browser
* Opera & Opera GX
* Vivaldi
* Arc for Windows & Chromium

### Does PilPod slow down my PC or consume heavy memory?
Not at all. PilPod Desktop is engineered with native Windows efficiency and stays lightweight. The floating corner widget operates with zero runtime overhead, and the companion extension only communicates when media events occur.

### Does PilPod track my browsing history or media consumption?
**Absolutely not.** PilPod operates under a strict **100% local-first privacy policy**. There are zero external telemetry servers, zero analytics trackers, and zero cloud accounts. All communication between your browsers and PilPod happens strictly on your local PC (`127.0.0.1`).

### Is PilPod free?
Yes! PilPod provides its complete core experience — multi-browser media control, volume boost, floating widget, tab hub, and local vault — completely free.

### How do I install and set up PilPod?
1. Download and run the **PilPod Desktop installer** for Windows.
2. Launch PilPod — it will automatically detect your installed browsers.
3. Click to install the **PilPod Companion extension** from your browser's extension store.
4. You're all set! Start playing any music or video and watch it instantly appear in PilPod.
